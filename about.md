I am a master's student at [ETH Zurich](https://ethz.ch/en.html), studying computer science. I aspire to creative practice involving photography, graphic design, and interactive media; thus my research focuses on **developing intuitive computational methods that assist artists and designers**. I completed by bachelor's degree in computer science and cognitive science at the [University of Toronto](https://www.utoronto.ca).

I am grateful to have worked as a research assistant at the [Dynamic Graphics Project](https://www.dgp.toronto.edu) Lab, advised by [Prof. Karan Singh](https://www.dgp.toronto.edu/~karan/) and [Dr. Karran Pandey](https://karranpandey.github.io). Previously, I was also fortunate to develop a [compact method for producing film-like colours from digital images](https://musicofmusix.github.io/siggraphposters25), under the supervision of [Prof. Michael Brown](http://www.cse.yorku.ca/~mbrown) and [Dr. Hakki Karaimer](https://karaimer.github.io), at [Samsung AI Center - Toronto](https://research.samsung.com/aicenter_toronto).
