I am a forth-year undergraduate student majoring Computer Science and Cognitive Science at the [University of Toronto](https://web.cs.toronto.edu).
I enjoy all things visually pleasing: especially photography, graphic design, and 3D rendering. Because of that, I like to spend my time either making art myself, or the tools help others do the same.
This year, I was lucky to be able to work on a [method for producing film-like colours from digital images](https://musicofmusix.github.io/siggraphposters25), under the supervision of [Prof. Michael Brown](http://www.cse.yorku.ca/~mbrown) and [Dr. Hakki Karaimer](https://karaimer.github.io), at [Samsung AI Center - Toronto](https://research.samsung.com/aicenter_toronto), published as a poster for [SIGGRAPH 2025](https://s2025.siggraph.org).
Additionally, I am interested in the human perception of aethetics. For example, what makes a good image? Why do different "tastes" arise? In a landscape where improving objective qualities (noise, resolution, etc.) is well-developed, I believe that understanding subjective qualities is the logical next step.
