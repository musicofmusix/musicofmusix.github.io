abstract: >
 This project consists of an interactive 3D scene of a train platform and an animated train, rendered realtime in the Unity game engine. Heavily inspired by the 1995 movie "Whisper of the Heart", the visual style replicates the 1990s Japanese hand-drawn animation style. Selective, high contrast cel shading, black outlines for edges, and strong bloom were used as primary methods. Part of an in-person interactive art exhibition in Gimpo, South Korea, 2023.

# Exhibition Installation 
<img src="/assets/img/metro/irl.webp" class="w-full sm:w-2/3 h-auto mx-auto">

The project was installed as part of an introduction sequence for an in-person interactive art exhibition in Gimpo, South Korea.
The virtual metro train would "arrive" when visitors were detected entering, via an Xbox Kinect sensor.
