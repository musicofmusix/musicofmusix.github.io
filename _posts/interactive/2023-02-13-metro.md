abstract: |
  Metro is an interactive and animated scene of a train arriving to its station, rendered in real-time in the Unity game engine. The visual theme is heavily inspired by the 1995 movie "Whisper of the Heart", replicating the classic Japanese hand-drawn animation style. High contrast toon and edge outline shaders are used as primary techniques. The train arrives and departs upon user input or detected movement from an Xbox Kinect sensor. Part of an in-person interactive art exhibition in Gimpo, South Korea, 2023.

# Exhibition Installation 
<img src="/assets/img/metro/irl.webp" class="w-full h-auto mx-auto">

The project was installed as part of an introduction sequence for an in-person interactive art exhibition in Gimpo, South Korea.
The virtual metro train would "arrive" when visitors were detected entering, via an Xbox Kinect sensor.
